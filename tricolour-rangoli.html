Tricolour Rangoli

An automated Rangoli and Kolam drawing device designed to create intricate and symmetrical floor art using three different colours of powder simultaneously

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The desired Rangoli or Kolam pattern is selected and loaded into the device.

  2. 02

    The device follows the programmed design path to draw the pattern automatically.

  3. 03

    The multi-color dispensing system releases colored powders along the designated paths.

What it is

The device follows a predefined design path to reproduce intricate Rangoli and Kolam patterns while coordinating the controlled dispensing of three different coloured powders. Its automated movement and multi-colour dispensing mechanism enable the creation of symmetrical designs with uniform powder distribution and reduced manual intervention.

Key features

Automated drawing of intricate Rangoli and Kolam patterns

Simultaneous dispensing of three different colours

Precise movement for uniform and symmetrical designs
