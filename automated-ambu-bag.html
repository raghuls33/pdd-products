Automated Ambu Bag

An automated emergency breathing support system designed to provide controlled airflow to patients by mechanically compressing an Ambu bag

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The patient is connected to a breathing circuit using an Ambu bag.

  2. 02

    The device mechanically compresses the Ambu bag to deliver airflow to the patient.

  3. 03

    The automated mechanism repeats the compression and release cycles to support continuous breathing assistance.

What it is

It helps maintain consistent breathing cycles and provides audio alerts for safety indications, reducing the physical effort required for manual bag compression during emergency situations.

Key features

Automated Ambu bag compression for breathing support

Consistent and repetitive compression cycles

Hands-free mechanical operation to reduce manual effort

Portable design for emergency and patient transport settings

Where it's used

Emergency wards, ambulances, rural health centers, disaster relief camps, temporary medical facilities, and patient transport environments where emergency breathing support may be required.

Benefits

  • Reduces the need for continuous manual Ambu bag compression
  • Helps provide repetitive and controlled breathing assistance
  • Reduces physical fatigue for healthcare providers during emergencies
  • Provides audio alerts to improve awareness of device status
  • Supports emergency response in resource-limited healthcare settings
  • Offers a practical solution for temporary breathing assistance during patient transit
