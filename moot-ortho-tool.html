Multi Utility Ortho Tool (MOOT)

A compact and portable physiotherapy assessment workstation designed to organize and securely hold essential diagnostic and measurement tools in one convenient setup.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The clinician stores the essential physiotherapy assessment tools in their designated holders.

  2. 02

    The required instrument can be accessed quickly whenever a patient assessment is needed.

  3. 03

    The clinician uses the tools to evaluate joint range of motion, body measurements, reflex responses, and other relevant physical parameters.

What it is

The workstation provides dedicated storage and holders for essential assessment instruments, allowing clinicians to arrange, access, and manage diagnostic tools conveniently during clinical procedures.

Key features

Integrated storage for essential physiotherapy assessment instruments

Organized arrangement for quick and easy tool access

Compact and portable design for clinical mobility

Where it's used

Physiotherapy clinics Orthopedic outpatient departments (OPDs) Sports rehabilitation centers Hospitals Community health camps Rehabilitation facilities Home-visit physical therapy services

Benefits

  • Keeps essential assessment instruments organized in one place
  • Improves convenience and efficiency during patient evaluations
  • Reduces time spent searching for individual diagnostic tools
  • Supports smooth workflow during clinical practice and hospital rounds
  • Enables convenient transportation of assessment tools for field visits and home-based rehabilitation
  • Promotes better organization and accessibility of instruments during physiotherapy and rehabilitation procedures
