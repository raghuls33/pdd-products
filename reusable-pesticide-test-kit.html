Reusable Pesticide Test Kit

The Reusable Pesticide Test Kit is a portable testing solution designed to screen fruits and vegetables for selected pesticide residues.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    A sample of the fruit or vegetable is selected for testing.

  2. 02

    The sample surface is wiped using an acetone-wet wipe, following the kit's instructions.

  3. 03

    The test solution is applied to the reusable test slip, which is then swabbed over the sample.

  4. 04

    The test slip is observed for a color change after the recommended waiting period.

What it is

The kit uses a chemical reaction and color-change detection method to indicate the possible presence of specific pesticides, including glyphosate and monocrotophos. The kit includes a reusable test slip, test solution, gloves, and acetone wet wipes for convenient sample testing.

Key features

Reusable test slip for repeated testing, as specified by the manufacturer

Color-change-based pesticide screening

Designed to screen for selected pesticides, including glyphosate anmonocrotophos

Where it's used

Households, fruit and vegetable markets, agricultural research institutions, food quality awareness programs, agricultural extension services, and educational laboratories conducting demonstrations of pesticide screening in produce.

Benefits

  • Provides a convenient method for preliminary pesticide screening
  • Supports greater awareness of potential pesticide residues on produce
  • Enables on-site testing without routine laboratory equipment
  • Offers a simple procedure for educational and demonstration purposes
