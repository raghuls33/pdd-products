Hydrogel-Based Adaptive Splint

A Hydrogel-Based Adaptive Splint is a hand rehabilitation device designed to support injury recovery, controlled hand movement, and rehabilitation.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user wears the splint to support the hand during rehabilitation.

  2. 02

    The splint assists with controlled hand movements and guided exercises.

  3. 03

    The hydrogel layer helps maintain a moist environment to support wound healing, when suitable for the wound and applied appropriately.

  4. 04

    The device supports rehabilitation activities and recovery under suitable healthcare guidance.

What it is

The device combines a supportive splint with hydrogel-based care to help maintain a moist environment that supports wound healing when appropriately formulated and applied.

Key features
