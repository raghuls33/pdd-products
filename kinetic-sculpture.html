How it works

  1. 01

    The system's control board executes pre-programmed motion sequences and rhythmic routines.

  2. 02

    Precision motors and mechanical linkages drive the physical sculpture elements to move in fluid harmony.

  3. 03

    Integrated sensors allow the sculpture to react dynamically to viewer proximity or environmental changes.
