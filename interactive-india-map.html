Interactive India Map

An interactive India map puzzle designed to help users explore Indian geography, states, capitals, languages, and regional culture through hands-on learning

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user selects and places a state puzzle piece on the corresponding location on the India map.

  2. 02

    The device identifies the state placed on the board.

  3. 03

    It announces the state name, capital, language, and key regional facts through audio feedback.

What it is

The system enables users to identify and place individual state puzzle pieces in their corresponding locations on the map of India. Upon detecting the placement of a state, the device provides audio information about its name, capital, language, and other relevant regional facts.

Key features

Interactive state identification through puzzle placement

Audio-based learning about states, capitals, languages, and regional facts

Learn and quiz modes for interactive educational experiences

Instant audio feedback for an engaging, screen-free learning experience

Where it's used

Ideal for school social studies classrooms, geography laboratories, STEM innovation centers, children's activity libraries, educational institutions, and competitive exam training centers. It supports hands-on geography learning, general knowledge development, and interactive educational activities.

Benefits

  • Makes learning Indian geography fun, interactive, and engaging
  • Helps users remember state locations, capitals, and languages
  • Promotes hands-on learning and spatial awareness
  • Encourages independent exploration and self-paced learning
  • Reinforces geographical knowledge through quiz-based practice
  • Provides an audio-guided, screen-free educational experience
