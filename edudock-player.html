EduDock Player

An interactive learning device designed especially for children, shaped like a miniature television.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The child selects a learning card based on their interest or learning topic.

  2. 02

    The card is placed on the device to access the associated content.

  3. 03

    The device displays relevant visuals and plays audio, such as songs, stories, and educational lessons.

What it is

A smart device which allows kids to explore songs, stories, and educational content in a fun and engaging way. By using different learning cards, children can access a variety of audio and visual experiences that make learning enjoyable, interactive, and easy to understand.

Key features

Interactive card-based learning experience

Educational songs, stories, and learning activities

Child-friendly, miniature television design

Where it's used

Preschools Early childhood education centers Activity centers Kindergarten classrooms Home learning environments

Benefits

  • Makes learning fun, engaging, and interactive for children
  • Supports language development and listening comprehension
  • Encourages curiosity, imagination, and independent learning
  • Improves memory, recognition, and cognitive skills
  • Provides an enjoyable way to explore educational content
  • Promotes early learning through songs, stories, and visual experiences
