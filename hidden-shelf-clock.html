Hidden Shelf Clock

The Hidden Digital Clock Wooden Shelf is a decorative furniture solution that integrates a digital time display into an elegant wooden surface.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The device is powered on to activate the concealed digital clock display.

  2. 02

    The built-in clock system maintains accurate real-time timekeeping.

  3. 03

    Soft illuminated digits shine through the wooden surface to display the current time.

  4. 04

    The time remains clearly visible while the shelf maintains its clean, minimalist decorative appearance.

What it is

An elegant decorative wooden shelf with a seamlessly integrated hidden digital clock. The time illuminates through the fine wooden surface, creating a sleek and sophisticated display without visible electronic components.
