Push-up Counter

A smart push-up counting device designed to automatically count repetitions and track completed sets during exercise.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places the device on the floor in front of them before starting the exercise.

  2. 02

    The device detects the user's downward movement during each push-up.

  3. 03

    It automatically counts each repetition without requiring manual input.

  4. 04

    The display shows the live repetition count and the number of completed sets.

What it is

The device detects each push-up as the user lowers their body toward the device and displays the live repetition count, helping users monitor their performance and maintain a structured workout routine.

Key features

Automatic push-up repetition counting

Real-time display of repetition count

Tracking of completed exercise sets

Customizable repetition targets

Portable design for convenient use

Where it's used

Home workouts Gyms Fitness centers Sports training academies School College physical education programs Military Police physical training Physiotherapy or rehabilitation exercise tracking

Benefits

  • Provides accurate and convenient push-up repetition tracking
  • Eliminates the need for manual counting
  • Helps users maintain structured workout routines
  • Encourages consistency and motivation through target-based exercise
  • Enables easy monitoring of workout progress and performance
  • Supports fitness training, physical education, and rehabilitation activities
