Interactive Periodic Table

An interactive periodic table designed to help users explore chemical elements in a simple, engaging, and educational way.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user selects a chemical element by pressing its corresponding button on the table.

  2. 02

    The system identifies the selected element and retrieves its relevant information.

  3. 03

    The element’s details, including its symbol, atomic number, and properties, are displayed on the screen.

What it is

An interactive table which allows users to select individual elements and learn about their symbols, atomic numbers, properties, and other important information through audio feedback and on-screen displays

Key features

Interactive selection of individual chemical elements

Instant display of element information and properties

Audio-based explanation of chemical elements

Simple and engaging hands-on learning experience

Where it's used

School science laboratories Chemistry classrooms STEM discovery centers Educational museums Science exhibitions Interactive learning programs

Benefits

  • Makes learning the periodic table more interactive and enjoyable
  • Helps students understand chemical elements and their properties
  • Improves engagement through hands-on interaction and audio learning
  • Supports visual and auditory learning methods
  • Encourages curiosity and exploration in chemistry and science
  • Simplifies access to essential information about chemical elements
