Smart Power Plug

A Smart Power Plug is an intelligent electrical monitoring device designed to measure and display the current, voltage, power consumption, and estimated electricity cost of connected appliances.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user connects an electrical appliance to one of the available power sockets.

  2. 02

    The device measures the electrical current and voltage consumed by the connected appliance.

  3. 03

    It calculates the power consumption and estimates the electricity cost based on energy usage and the configured tariff.

  4. 04

    The measured values are displayed on the OLED screen for easy reference.

  5. 05

    The same information is sent to a mobile application, allowing users to monitor electrical usage conveniently.

  6. 06

    The user can operate the power plug using the available switches.

What it is

It provides users with a convenient way to monitor electrical usage through both an OLED display and a mobile application, helping them understand and manage their energy consumption.

Key features

Real-time current and voltage monitoring

Electrical power consumption measurement

Estimated electricity cost display

OLED display for local monitoring

Mobile application for remote monitoring

Where it's used

Homes Offices Laboratories Educational institutions Energy monitoring projects Small-scale electrical appliance monitoring

Benefits

  • Provides convenient monitoring through both a display and mobile application Helps estimate electricity costs
  • Supports energy-conscious usage of electrical appliances
  • Makes electrical usage information easily accessible
  • Encourages better energy management and awareness
