Smart Progressive Resistance Trainer

The Smart Progressive Resistance Trainer is a compact exercise and rehabilitation device with adjustable resistance for controlled muscle strengthening.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The therapist or user sets the resistance level.

  2. 02

    The user performs the prescribed exercise against that resistance.

  3. 03

    As strength improves, the resistance is raised for gradual, progressive overload.

What it is

A small resistance-training unit that replaces a rack of weights with one adjustable device. Users can step resistance up as they get stronger.

Key features

Adjustable resistance across several intensity levels

Controlled, steady resistance for safe strengthening

Compact footprint for clinics and homes

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Strength training General fitness

Benefits

  • Supports gradual, measurable improvement in muscle strength
  • One device covers many exercise levels
  • Controlled resistance makes it suitable for patients early in recovery
