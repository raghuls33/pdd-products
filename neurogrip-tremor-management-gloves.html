NeuroGrip Tremor Management Gloves

NeuroGrip is a wearable tremor management device designed to help people with hand tremors perform everyday activities with greater comfort and control.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user wears the glove on the affected hand.

  2. 02

    The device provides gentle vibrations to the hand.

  3. 03

    The user practises controlled hand movements while wearing the glove.

  4. 04

    The device is intended to support improved comfort and control during daily activities.

What it is

A specially designed glove that provides gentle vibrations to help manage hand tremors. Its wearable design allows users to use the device while practising everyday hand movements.

Key features

Wearable design for hand tremor management

Vibration-based support

Compact and portable construction

Designed to support everyday hand activities

Where it's used

Rehabilitation centres Physiotherapy settings Assistive care Exploratory support for people experiencing hand tremors

Benefits

  • May help users manage hand tremors during selected activities
  • Supports hand movement practice
  • Provides a wearable and convenient approach to tremor management
  • May help users work towards greater independence in daily activities
