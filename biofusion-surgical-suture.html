BioFusion Surgical Suture

A biodegradable surgical suture material designed to support wound closure.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The suture material is used to approximate and hold wound edges together during closure.

  2. 02

    The suture provides mechanical support to the wound during the healing process.

  3. 03

    The formulation incorporates selected natural extracts alongside a polymer-based material.

  4. 04

    The material's performance depends on its strength, biocompatibility, degradation behavior, and suitability for the intended medical application.

What it is

The bio-based suture formulation containing materials such as aloe vera gel, turmeric, green tea extract, and polyvinyl alcohol. It is intended to explore the integration of natural ingredients with suture materials for potential wound-care applications.

Key features

Suture-based wound closure concept

Incorporation of selected natural extracts

Polymer-based material formulation

Designed for investigation in wound-care applications

Where it's used

Biomedical research Biomaterials development Tissue engineering studies Wound-care material research Educational projects involving the development Evaluation of alternative surgical suture formulations

Benefits

  • Supports research into alternative suture materials
  • Encourages exploration of natural extracts in biomaterial formulations
  • Provides a platform for studying material properties relevant to wound closure
  • Promotes investigation of biocompatibility and biodegradation characteristics
  • Contributes to educational and research activities in biomedical engineering
