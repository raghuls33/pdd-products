RFID Music Player

The RFID Music Card Player is an interactive audio device designed to play music and stories through contactless RFID card recognition.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user selects an RFID music card containing a song or audio recording.

  2. 02

    The card is placed on the designated area of the player.

  3. 03

    The player identifies the card and retrieves the corresponding audio.

  4. 04

    The selected music or story plays through the built-in speakers.

What it is

The device allows users to play music and stories simply by placing RFID music cards on the player, offering an interactive, nostalgic, and screen-free listening experience.

Key features

RFID-based music selection using physical cards

Supports up to 100 unique music cards

Classic gramophone-inspired vintage design

Simple and intuitive operation

Built-in speakers for music and storytelling

Where it's used

Homes Memory care facilities Early learning spaces Educational environments Children's activity areas Decorative audio displays for music Storytelling
