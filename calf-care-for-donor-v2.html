Calf Care for Donor V2

The Calf Massager for Blood Donors is a supportive healthcare device designed to provide gentle, rhythmic compression to the calf muscles to encourage blood circulation in the lower limbs. It aims to improve donor comfort during supervised recovery during blood donation.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The massager is placed on the donor's calves before donation begins.

  2. 02

    It delivers gentle massage while blood is drawn.

  3. 03

    The massage helps relax the legs and encourages circulation.

What it is

A massager that fits around the donor's calves while they sit or recline for donation. It applies gentle, rhythmic massage to the lower legs throughout the procedure.

Key features

Gentle calf massage designed around the donation chair

Controlled compression and release mechanism

Safety features, including pressure limits and an emergency stop

Where it's used

Blood donation centers and blood banks Supervised post-donation recovery areas Healthcare and wellness facilities

Benefits

  • Improves donor comfort during donation
  • Supports circulation in the legs, which may help donors keep a comfortable blood pressure after donating
  • Offers adjustable massage settings, depending on the design
