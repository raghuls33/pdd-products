Tonnie Box

A screen-free interactive learning device designed to introduce children to animals and wildlife through sound and storytelling.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The child selects an animal figurine and places it on the device.

  2. 02

    The device recognizes the selected animal automatically.

  3. 03

    It plays the corresponding animal sounds, stories, and interesting facts.

What it is

Children can place different animal figurines on the box to listen to animal sounds, exciting stories, and interesting facts about each animal.

Key features

Interactive animal figurines for hands-on learning

Automatic animal recognition

Audio playback of animal sounds, stories, and facts

Screen-free and child-friendly design

Where it's used

Early childhood education Preschools Pediatric waiting rooms Daycare centers Libraries Home play areas Wildlife learning programs

Benefits

  • Encourages curiosity about animals and nature
  • Develops listening skills and auditory learning
  • Stimulates imagination and creative thinking
  • Promotes tactile learning through interactive figurines
  • Provides an engaging alternative to screen-based entertainment
  • Supports early childhood education through playful exploration
