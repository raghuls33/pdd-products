Laser Fitness Tracker

The Laser Fitness Tracker is an interactive sports-training device that challenges athletes to reach randomly changing targets within a set time and tracks their performance.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The coach or athlete sets the time limit.

  2. 02

    Targets appear at random positions.

  3. 03

    The athlete moves to reach each target as fast as possible.

  4. 04

    The system records response speed, movement accuracy, targets completed and overall performance.

What it is

A reaction and agility trainer. Targets appear in random positions and the athlete must reach each one before time runs out, while the system scores every attempt.

Key features

Randomly changing target positions

Timed challenges

Tracks response speed, accuracy, completed targets and overall score

Where it's used

Sports training Athletic coaching Gyms Fitness centers

Benefits

  • Improves reaction time, agility, coordination and movement speed
  • Gives coaches objective performance data
  • Lets athletes see progress over time
