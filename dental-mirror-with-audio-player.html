Dental Mirror with Audio Player

A paediatric dental mirror designed to provide clear visibility during children’s oral examinations while helping young patients feel calm and comfortable.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The dentist uses the device to examine the child’s teeth and oral cavity.

  2. 02

    The device plays soothing children’s songs to create a friendly and relaxing environment.

  3. 03

    The combination of clear visibility and calming audio helps make dental examinations smoother and more comfortable for children.

What it is

The device enables dental professionals to examine the teeth and oral cavity while playing calming children's songs to create a more relaxed and welcoming environment. By combining conventional dental examination functionality with child-friendly audio interaction, the device aims to reduce dental anxiety, encourage patient cooperation

Key features

Built-in audio playback of soothing children’s songs

Child-friendly design to help reduce dental anxiety

Simple and convenient operation

Where it's used

Paediatric dental clinics General dental practices School dental screening camps Dental colleges Training institutes Community oral health programs for children

Benefits

  • Provides a clearer view of teeth during dental examinations
  • Helps reduce fear and anxiety in young patients
  • Encourages better cooperation during dental procedures
  • Creates a more pleasant and child-friendly dental experience
  • Supports smoother and more efficient dental checkups
  • Helps dental professionals deliver more comfortable oral healthcare for children
