BMI Display Device

A BMI (Body Mass Index) device designed to measure a person's weight and height and calculate their BMI. It provides a simple and convenient way to understand an individual's body mass category.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user stands on the device to measure their weight.

  2. 02

    The device measures the user's height automatically.

  3. 03

    It calculates the BMI based on the measured weight and height.

  4. 04

    The BMI result is displayed on the screen for easy reference.

What it is

A BMI (Body Mass Index) finding device designed to measure a person's weight and height and calculate their BMI. It provides a simple and convenient way to understand an individual's body mass category.

Key features

Automatic weight and height measurement

Instant BMI calculation

Portable design for convenient use

Where it's used

Healthcare centers Hospitals Fitness centers Schools Wellness programs Personal health monitoring

Benefits
