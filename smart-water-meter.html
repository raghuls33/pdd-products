Smart Water Meter

Smart Water Meter helps consumers track the amount of water used in apartments and community buildings. It updates water usage details on the user's mobile phone every day, helping people use water responsibly.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The meter keeps track of the water used by each consumer.

  2. 02

    The day's water usage is updated on the user's mobile phone.

  3. 03

    Consumers can check their water use and make better choices to avoid waste.

What it is

A water meter that measures how much water each household uses. It shares daily water usage details with the consumer on a mobile phone.

Key features

Daily water usage updates on the mobile phone

Tracks water use for each household

Makes water usage easy to check

Where it's used

Metropolitan cities Residential apartments Gated communities Community buildings

Benefits

  • Helps consumers understand their daily water usage
  • Encourages people to use water responsibly
  • Helps building management check water use in individual homes
