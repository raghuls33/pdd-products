Napkin Vending Machine

A Smart Sanitary Napkin Vending Machine designed to dispense menstrual hygiene products conveniently and efficiently.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user selects their preferred sanitary napkin brand and size using the selection panel.

  2. 02

    The device dispenses the selected sanitary napkin into the product retrieval bay.

  3. 03

    The display shows the available quantity of sanitary napkins for easy reference.

  4. 04

    The system updates the stock count on a dedicated webpage, allowing authorized personnel to monitor inventory and identify when refilling is required.

What it is

The napkin vending machine accommodates sanitary napkins from three different brands in two different sizes, allowing users to select products according to their preferences.

Key features

Dispenses sanitary napkins from three different brands and two different sizes

Dedicated storage chambers for organized product dispensing

On-screen display of available sanitary napkin quantities

Real-time stock updates.

Where it's used

Schools, colleges, corporate offices, public washrooms, hospitals, and hostel facilities to provide convenient access to menstrual hygiene products around the clock.

Benefits

  • Promotes menstrual hygiene by improving access to essential sanitary products
  • Offers users a convenient and discreet way to obtain sanitary napkins
  • Provides multiple brands and sizes to accommodate different user preferences
  • Enables real-time stock monitoring for efficient inventory management
  • Helps facility administrators identify low stock and plan timely refilling
  • Supports the development of hygienic, accessible, and user-friendly public facilities
