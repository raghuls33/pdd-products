Hydroponics

The Automated Soilless Farming System is an intelligent cultivation solution designed to optimize plant growth through automated irrigation, nutrient management, and continuous environmental monitoring.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The system continuously monitors the nutrient concentration, acidity (pH), and temperature of the water.

  2. 02

    A live camera feed enables users to observe plant growth and monitor crop conditions remotely.

  3. 03

    Sensor readings and system updates are transmitted to a cloud-based mobile platform, allowing users to monitor farming activities and system performance in real time.

What it is

An automated soil-less farming system designed to provide controlled crop nutrition, automated irrigation, and real-time plant monitoring.

Key features

Automated irrigation and water circulation

Continuous monitoring of nutrient concentration, pH, and water temperature

Remote crop surveillance through live visual monitoring

Mobile-based cloud monitoring and system updates

Where it's used

Smart vertical farms Urban indoor gardening setups Agricultural research institutions Greenhouse nurseries Educational laboratories Controlled-environment cultivation of leafy greens Specialty crops

Benefits

  • Reduces the need for manual farming supervision
  • Helps maintain consistent nutrient and water conditions for healthy plant growth
  • Supports efficient irrigation and improved root oxygenation
  • Enables convenient remote monitoring of crops and system operations
  • Promotes space-efficient cultivation in indoor and urban environments
  • Supports improved crop productivity and more consistent growing conditions
  • Provides a practical platform for smart agriculture, research, and agricultural education
