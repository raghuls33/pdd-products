Breathing Exercise Trainer

An interactive respiratory training device designed to guide users through structured breathing exercises using visual light patterns.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user selects a breathing mode using the control buttons.

  2. 02

    The device guides inhalation through rhythmic light patterns.

  3. 03

    The user holds their breath for the duration indicated by the visual and audio prompts.

  4. 04

    The device signals when to exhale, helping the user maintain a steady breathing rhythm.

What it is

It helps users follow controlled inhalation, breath-holding, and exhalation cycles, promoting better breathing awareness, relaxation, and stress management.

Key features

Interactive breathing guidance through rhythmic light patterns

Multiple breathing modes for structured training sessions

Simple button-based controls for easy operation

Where it's used

Wellness centers Yoga Mindfulness training Pulmonary therapy clinics Student relaxation corners Stress management programs Daily home-based deep breathing exercises

Benefits

  • Encourages controlled and mindful breathing patterns
  • Helps promote relaxation and reduce perceived stress
  • Supports breathing awareness and respiratory training
  • Provides clear visual and audio guidance for users
  • Makes structured breathing exercises easier to follow
  • Encourages regular breathing practice for overall wellness
