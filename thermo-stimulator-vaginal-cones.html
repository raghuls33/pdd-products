Thermo Stimulator Vaginal Cones

A Thermo Stimulator Vaginal Cone is a therapeutic device designed to support pelvic floor relaxation and dilation therapy by combining gentle warmth and low-intensity vibration.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user prepares the device for use according to the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional.

  2. 02

    The device provides controlled warmth to support relaxation of the pelvic floor muscles.

  3. 03

    Gentle vibration provides sensory stimulation to promote relaxation and comfort.

  4. 04

    The cone-shaped design supports gradual dilation therapy.

  5. 05

    The device is intended to support pelvic rehabilitation under appropriate medical guidance.

What it is

It aims to improve comfort during therapy, reduce muscle tightness, and help users feel more relaxed during pelvic rehabilitation.

Key features

Combines heat and vibration in a single therapeutic device.

Provides controlled warmth and gentle sensory stimulation.

Features an ergonomic, cone-shaped design for dilation therapy.

Designed with user comfort and ease of handling in mind.

Where it's used

Gynecology clinics Pelvic floor physiotherapy centers Rehabilitation programs Healthcare professional-guided home therapy

Benefits

  • Supports pelvic floor muscle relaxation.
  • Helps promote comfort during dilation therapy.
  • May help reduce anxiety and fear associated with pelvic rehabilitation.
  • Combines two therapeutic functions in one device.
  • Supports a more comfortable and consistent rehabilitation experience.
