Laser Engraver

The Laser Engraver is an automated engraving device designed to create precise designs, text, patterns, and images on various materials using a focused laser beam. It aims to provide accurate, repeatable, and customizable engraving through programmed movements of the laser module and a controlled motion system.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The material is placed securely on the engraving platform.

  2. 02

    A programmed design is sent to the laser engraving system.

  3. 03

    The laser moves along the required paths and marks the surface according to the design.

What it is

A Laser Engraver is an automated engraving device designed to create text, patterns, logos, and images on suitable material surfaces using a focused laser beam. It uses a computer-controlled motion system to produce precise and detailed engravings.

Key features

Computer-controlled engraving and motion system

Precise engraving of text, logos, and patterns

Emergency stop and protective safety enclosure

Where it's used

Personalized gifts and decorative products Wood Leather Compatible material engraving Product labelling and identification Craft workshops and small-scale manufacturing

Benefits

  • Produces accurate and detailed engravings
  • Enables customized designs and repeatable results
  • Improves productivity for small-scale engraving tasks
