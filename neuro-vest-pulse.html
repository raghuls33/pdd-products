Neuro Vest Pulse

A wearable knee sleeve with a built-in pulse sensing unit that tracks the pulse in the knee region.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The soft knee sleeve is worn comfortably over the knee.

  2. 02

    The sensing patch rests against the skin and picks up the pulse in the knee region.

  3. 03

    The tracker unit processes the signal and presents the pulse reading.

  4. 04

    The user or therapist reviews the readings to follow changes over time.

  5. 05

    Simple to use with no special setup

What it is

A wearable knee sleeve with a built-in pulse sensing unit that tracks the pulse in the knee region. It gives a simple, non-invasive way to monitor knee-area pulse activity during daily use, exercise, or recovery.

Key features

Pulse tracking directly in the knee region

Soft, stretchable sleeve for a comfortable, secure fit

Compact, lightweight tracker unit

Non-invasive, easy to wear and remove

Simple to use with no special setup

Where it's used

Physiotherapy clinics Post-operative knee recovery Sports injury rehabilitation Orthopedic care centers Geriatric care Home-based monitoring

Benefits

  • Helps track knee-area pulse changes during recovery
  • Supports therapists and patients with objective readings
  • Comfortable enough for extended wear
  • Encourages safer, better-informed rehabilitation
