Pharmacy Rack

A smart pharmacy rack identity system provides a seamless and efficient way to manage inventory and retrieve stock quickly.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    A medicine or item is selected through the system interface.

  2. 02

    The control board identifies the corresponding storage rack location.

  3. 03

    Integrated addressable LED strips light up to point directly to the correct shelf and bin.

  4. 04

    The item is easily located and retrieved without manual searching.

What it is

An automated smart pharmacy rack system designed to organize medicines and guide store personnel or patients to the exact location of required items using vibrant LED indicators. It provides a seamless and efficient way to manage inventory and retrieve stock quickly.

Key features

Automated rack and shelf location guidance

Visual status indicators using color-coded lighting

Real-time item searching and tracking

Scalable multi-rack organization layout

Where it's used

Pharmacies Hospitals Medical stores Healthcare facilities Automated inventory warehouses

Benefits

  • Speeds up medicine retrieval and prescription ful-fillment
  • Eliminates searching errors and misplaced items
  • Supports efficient inventory management and stock organization
  • Reduces operational time for staff and improves customer service
