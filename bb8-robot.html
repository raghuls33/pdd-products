What it is

BB-8 is a smart, interactive robotic companion inspired by the iconic Star Wars droid. Featuring a spherical body and a dome-shaped head, it is designed to move smoothly, respond to user commands, and provide an engaging and entertaining robotic experience.

Key features

Interactive movement and wireless control

Unique spherical body with a dome-shaped head

Smooth and engaging robotic motion

Demonstrates robotics, automation, and control concepts
