Smart Pet Plant

A Smart Pet Plant Companion designed to monitor indoor plant health and express plant needs through interactive feedback

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    Integrated environmental sensors continuously measure soil moisture, ambient temperature, humidity, and light exposure.

  2. 02

    Internal algorithms evaluate the plant's health metrics against optimal growing conditions.

  3. 03

    The system communicates the plant's status and health feedback in real-time through an interactive display or companion app.

What it is

A Smart Pet Plant Companion designed to monitor indoor plant health, automate hydration, and express plant needs through interactive feedback. It provides an engaging and effortless way to nurture indoor plants while keeping owners connected to their plant's wellbeing.

Key features

Automated precision soil hydration and water level management

Multi-parameter health tracking (moisture, temperature, and light levels)

Expressive status display giving the plant a personality

Where it's used

Homes Office desks Indoor gardens Apartments Classrooms Commercial spaces

Benefits

  • Prevents overwatering or underwatering by delivering exact moisture needs
  • Makes plant care effortless and worry-free, even during extended travel
  • Creates a fun, interactive personal connection between plant lovers and their plants
  • Promotes healthier indoor growth and prevents plant neglect.
