What it is

A lightweight, portable medicine cooling box designed to keep temperature-sensitive medicines, such as insulin, within the recommended cooling range. It helps protect medicines from external heat and maintains a suitable storage environment during travel and everyday outdoor activities.

Key features

Portable and lightweight design for easy transportation

Continuous internal temperature monitoring

Automatic temperature regulation

Where it's used

Ideal for people using insulin, travellers, healthcare professionals, clinics, and emergency medical workers who need to carry and protect temperature-sensitive medicines. It is suitable for everyday outdoor activities, journeys, and situations where access to conventional refrigeration may be limited.

Benefits

  • Helps maintain suitable storage conditions for temperature-sensitive medicines
  • Reduces the risk of medicine spoilage caused by excessive heat
  • Enables convenient and reliable medicine transportation
  • Supports continuous temperature monitoring and better storage management
  • Provides a practical cooling solution for travel and outdoor healthcare needs
