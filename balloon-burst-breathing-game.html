Balloon Burst Breathing Game

The Balloon Burst Breathing Game encourages controlled, powerful blowing by inflating and bursting an on-screen balloon based on the user's effort.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user blows into the device.

  2. 02

    Blowing effort is measured and shown as a balloon inflating on screen.

  3. 03

    Enough sustained effort bursts the balloon, giving a clear goal and reward.

What it is

A visual breathing-exercise game. The harder and more steadily the user blows, the more the balloon inflates, until it bursts.

Key features

Effort-driven balloon visual

Clear target that motivates stronger breaths

Simple to understand at any age

Where it's used

Respiratory physiotherapy Breathing exercises Pulmonary rehabilitation Patient therapy

Benefits

  • Builds breath control and respiratory strength
  • Gives instant visual feedback on effort
  • Makes rehabilitation sessions more engaging
