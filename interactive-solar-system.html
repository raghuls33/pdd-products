Interactive Solar System

A Planetary Model is an interactive, motorized educational device designed to demonstrate what planets present around the Sun.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user places the planetary model.

  2. 02

    The device provides educational information about planetary sizes, orbital speeds, distances from the Sun, and compositions through audio explanations.

  3. 03

    Users can explore the solar system in study mode or test their knowledge through interactive quizzes with instant feedback.

What it is

The system provides an engaging way to explore the solar system through audio explanations, and interactive learning activities.

Key features

Audio-based educational facts about the solar system

Interactive study mode

Instant feedback to support engaging and effective learning

Hands-on, screen-free astronomy learning experience

Where it's used

School science laboratories Planetariums STEM innovation centers Astronomy clubs Educational institutions Interactive museum exhibits

Benefits

  • Provides a visual and interactive understanding of planetary motion
  • Helps learners explore planetary characteristics and the structure of the solar system
  • Makes astronomy education engaging through synchronized movement and audio learning
  • Encourages curiosity, active participation, and experiential learning
  • Reinforces scientific knowledge through interactive quizzes and instant feedback
  • Supports screen-free learning and improves the overall educational experience
