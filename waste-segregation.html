Waste Segregation

The Waste Segregation System is designed to identify and separate waste into appropriate categories, helping make waste handling more organized and supporting recycling and responsible disposal.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places waste into the system's input area.

  2. 02

    Sensors detect selected properties of the item, such as material or metal presence, depending on the design.

  3. 03

    A controller operates a flap, gate or actuator to direct the item into the selected bin.

What it is

A system that uses sensors and a controlled mechanism to detect selected waste characteristics and direct items into separate collection bins. The exact categories depend on the sensors and mechanism used.

Key features

Separate collection compartments

Automatic sorting mechanism

Expandable design for additional waste categories

Where it's used

Homes Schools Offices Public spaces Laboratories Small waste-collection points

Benefits

  • Encourages waste separation at the point of disposal
  • Can reduce manual sorting effort
  • Helps keep recyclable and non-recyclable waste organized
  • Supports cleaner waste-management practices
