Camera Slider

A smooth-motion mobile camera slider designed to move a smartphone horizontally along a straight track for stable, cinematic video recording.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The mobile phone is securely mounted on the holder attached to the slider.

  2. 02

    The device moves the phone horizontally along the track.

  3. 03

    The movement can be controlled to glide left or right at the desired pace.

  4. 04

    The smooth motion helps capture steady panning, tracking, and reveal shots with minimal camera shake.

What it is

The device allows users to capture controlled camera movements, creating professional-looking footage with smooth transitions and consistent motion.

Key features

Smooth and controlled horizontal camera movement

Motorized operation for consistent motion

Adjustable left and right movement

Secure mobile phone mounting

Simple operation for repeatable camera shots
