BioFresh Odour Control Bio Patches

BioFresh Odour Control Bio Pads are enzyme-powered deodorizing pads designed to help control unpleasant odours in household and waste-management areas

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places a BioFresh pad inside a dustbin, beneath a bin liner, or near an odour-prone area.

  2. 02

    The pad releases its deodorizing components to help control unpleasant smells.

  3. 03

    The enzyme-based formulation and beneficial microbes are intended to support odour control at the source.

What it is

The pads contain neem extract, vetiver powder, natural fragrance, and beneficial microbes, as stated on the packaging. Their biodegradable design provides a convenient solution for managing odours in areas where waste, moisture, and organic residues accumulate.

Key features

Enzyme-powered odour control formulation

Contains neem extract and vetiver powder

Includes natural fragrance and beneficial microbes

Biodegradable pad design, as stated on the packaging

Where it's used

Household dustbins, garbage containers, kitchen sinks, wet-waste bins, washrooms, drains, and other household areas prone to unpleasant odours. The pads are suitable for general household odour-management applications.

Benefits

  • Helps reduce unpleasant household odours
  • Supports odour control near waste and organic residues
  • Provides a convenient alternative to frequent spray-based deodorizing
  • Offers easy placement without complex installation
