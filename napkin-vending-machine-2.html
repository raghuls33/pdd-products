Napkin Vending Machine

A sanitary napkin vending machine designed to provide quick, convenient, and private access to menstrual hygiene products.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user views the available sanitary napkin options on the display.

  2. 02

    The user selects the napkin for dispensing.

  3. 03

    The machine processes the selection and dispenses the chosen sanitary napkin.

  4. 04

    The user collects the product quickly and privately, without any assistance.

What it is

The device dispenses the selected sanitary napkin instantly, without requiring assistance, promoting menstrual hygiene and accessibility in public and private spaces.

Key features

Simple and user-friendly selection process

Quick and automatic product dispensing

Private and independent access to menstrual hygiene products

Convenient product refilling for continuous availability

Where it's used

Schools Colleges Women's hostels Offices Corporate campuses Hospitals Railway stations Bus stands Shopping malls Public restrooms Community hygiene programs

Benefits

  • Provides quick and convenient access to sanitary napkins
  • Promotes menstrual hygiene and personal well-being
  • Ensures privacy, comfort, and independence for users
  • Offers multiple product choices to suit individual needs
  • Reduces dependence on manual distribution or staff assistance
  • Supports better menstrual hygiene management in public and institutional spaces
