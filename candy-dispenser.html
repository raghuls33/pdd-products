Candy Dispenser

The Interactive Candy Dispenser is an educational device designed to make mathematics learning engaging through interactive quizzes and reward-based activities

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The child interacts with the device and receives a simple mathematics question.

  2. 02

    The child solves the problem and enters the answer using the input interface.

  3. 03

    The device checks the answer and provides feedback based on the response.

  4. 04

    Upon answering correctly, the child receives a candy reward.

What it is

The device presents mathematical questions, accepts user responses, and provides immediate feedback. When a correct answer is entered, the system dispenses a candy as a reward

Key features

Interactive mathematics-based learning activities

Reward-based system to encourage participation

Instant feedback on answers

Engaging and child-friendly learning experience

Combines education with fun and motivation

Where it's used

Elementary schools STEM activity centers Learning laboratories Educational institutions Home study desks to promote regular mathematics practice Encourage independent learning Make number-based activities more engaging

Benefits

  • Makes learning mathematics enjoyable through interactive gameplay
  • Improves calculation speed and accuracy
  • Develops logical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Encourages active participation and consistent practice
  • Builds confidence in solving mathematical problems
  • Motivates children through rewards and positive reinforcement
