Ferrie Iron Bar for Pregnant Women

Ferrie is an iron-enriched nutritional bar specially formulated for pregnant women.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user consumes the nutritional bar as part of their regular dietary routine.

  2. 02

    The iron content contributes to the body's iron intake, which is important for haemoglobin production.

  3. 03

    Dietary fibre supports normal digestive function.

  4. 04

    The added postbiotics are intended to support gut health.

What it is

Ferrie has iron, dietary fibre, postbiotics, and essential nutrients from selected ingredients such as moringa, beetroot, curry leaves, and other plant-based sources. The bar is designed to provide a convenient nutritional supplement as part of a balanced diet during pregnancy.

Key features

Iron-enriched nutritional formulation

High dietary fibre content

Postbiotics to support gut health

Plant-derived ingredients, including moringa and beetroot

Where it's used

Maternal nutrition programmes, prenatal wellness initiatives, nutritional education, dietary supplementation planning, and healthcare settings focused on improving awareness of nutritional needs during pregnancy.

Benefits

  • Provides a convenient source of dietary iron
  • Contributes to daily dietary fibre intake
  • Supports nutritional intake during pregnancy
  • Offers a portable and ready-to-consume snack option
