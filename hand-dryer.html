Hand Dryer

A touch-free automatic hand dryer designed to dry users’ hands hygienically within 5 to 8 seconds.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places their hands inside the designated hand insertion area.

  2. 02

    The device automatically detects the presence of the user's hands.

  3. 03

    A high-speed airflow is activated to dry the hands quickly and efficiently.

  4. 04

    The drying process is completed within 5 to 8 seconds.

What it is

The hand dryer eliminates the need for paper towels, providing a convenient and eco-friendly hand-drying solution. It is suitable for high-traffic environments such as restaurants, banquet halls, and public washrooms, ensuring efficient operation and improved washroom hygiene.

Key features

Touch-free hand detection and automatic operation

Rapid hand drying within 5 to 8 seconds

High-speed airflow for efficient moisture removal

Hygienic and convenient alternative to paper towels

Where it's used

Institutional washrooms Commercial dining spaces Restaurants Banquet Marriage halls Colleges Hospitals Office campuses Other public facilities requiring efficient hand drying Hygienic sanitation

Benefits

  • Reduces paper waste and ongoing consumable costs
  • Supports efficient operation in high-traffic environments
  • Minimizes maintenance requirements associated with paper towel disposal and replenishment
  • Provides a quick, convenient, and user-friendly hand-drying experience
