Enzyme Stain Removal Cleaning Spray

An Enzyme-Based Stain Removal Cleaning Spray designed to remove tough stains from various surfaces using enzymes such as amylase, protease, and lipase.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user shakes the bottle before use to mix the cleaning solution.

  2. 02

    The spray is applied to the stained area according to the product instructions.

  3. 03

    The enzymes help break down different types of stain residues, including starch-, protein-, and fat-based stains.

  4. 04

    The treated area is wiped using the supplied microfiber cloth.

What it is

The spray is plant-waste-derived and is intended to provide a convenient cleaning solution with reduced water requirements. The product comes with a microfiber cloth to support effective stain removal and surface cleaning.

Key features

Enzyme-based stain removal formulation

Contains amylase, protease, and lipase

Plant-waste-derived cleaning concept

Suitable for multiple surface-cleaning applications

Convenient spray bottle for easy application

Where it's used

Households, kitchens, offices, educational institutions, cleaning services, and other environments requiring routine stain removal from suitable surfaces. It can be used for cleaning demonstrations and projects exploring enzyme-based, plant-derived cleaning formulations.

Benefits

  • Helps remove tough stains through enzyme-based cleaning
  • Offers convenient spray-and-wipe application
  • Supports reduced-water cleaning practices
  • Combines cleaning solution and microfiber cloth in one package
