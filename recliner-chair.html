Recliner Chair

A WiFi-Enabled Recliner Chair is a smart seating device designed to provide convenient and effortless control over reclining, lifting, and seating adjustments through a wireless network.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user accesses the chair's dedicated web interface through a WiFi-enabled device.

  2. 02

    The user selects the desired reclining, lifting, or adjustment function.

  3. 03

    The chair responds to the commands and adjusts its position using its motorized mechanism.

What it is

The recliner chair has motorized mechanism responds to user commands, enabling smooth and effortless position adjustments. In addition to wireless operation, the chair includes manual control buttons for direct operation when required.

Key features

Wireless control through a dedicated web interface

Motorized reclining and lifting adjustments

Convenient remote operation for users and caregivers

Manual override for direct control

Where it's used

Smart homes Assisted living facilities Hospitals Senior care environments Rehabilitation centers Post-surgery recovery spaces Home-based mobility assistance

Benefits

  • Provides effortless control of chair positioning and reclining
  • Enhances comfort and convenience for elderly users and individuals with limited mobility
  • Enables caregivers to adjust the chair remotely when needed
  • Supports improved accessibility and independence in daily activities
  • Promotes comfortable seating and posture adjustments during rest and recovery
