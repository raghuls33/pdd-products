Food Serving Robot

The Food Serving Robot is a mobile multi-tier trolley robot that carries food and other items from one point to another, reducing manual serving effort.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    Food or items are placed on the trays.

  2. 02

    The robot is started and moves on its wheeled base to the serving point.

  3. 03

    The items are collected from the trays on arrival.

  4. 04

    The robot is then ready for the next serving trip.

What it is

A wheeled robot with a compact black base and three stacked trays on a steel support column. The trays hold plates, bowls or other items while the robot moves them to where they are needed.

Key features

Three-tier tray design for carrying more items at once

Wheeled mobile base for smooth movement

Strong steel column with a stable build

Compact and easy to operate

Where it's used

Hospitals Hostels Canteens Restaurants Hotels Offices

Benefits

  • Reduces the walking and carrying effort of staff
  • Serves several items in a single trip
  • Saves time and keeps serving consistent in busy places
