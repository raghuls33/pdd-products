Roller Massager

The Roller Massager is a portable foam roller with built-in vibration that gives deeper relaxation and helps relieve muscle stiffness and soreness.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user switches on the vibration.

  2. 02

    They roll the target muscle group (calves, thighs, back) over the roller.

  3. 03

    Vibration and pressure combine to loosen tight muscle and ease soreness.

What it is

A foam roller that adds powered vibration to standard rolling. The vibration works alongside the user's body weight to reach deeper into tight muscle than rolling alone.

Key features

Built-in vibration for deeper muscle relaxation

Portable, lightweight design for use at home, clinic or gym

Works on major muscle groups

Where it's used

Physiotherapy sessions Fitness warm-ups Cool-downs Rehabilitation programs

Benefits

  • Reduces muscle stiffness and post-exercise soreness
  • Supports faster muscle recovery
  • Gives patients a self-care tool they can use between therapy sessions
