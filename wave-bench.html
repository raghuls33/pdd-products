What it is

The wave-pattern structure is designed to follow the natural form of the body, providing a comfortable seating surface and promoting a relaxed sitting posture. The curved seating profile helps distribute body weight across the seating surface, enhancing comfort during extended periods of use.

Key features

Ergonomic wave-pattern design for enhanced comfort and posture support

Even weight distribution for a balanced seating experience
