Dumbbell Alarm Clock

A motivational alarm clock designed in the shape of a dumbbell that transforms waking up into an active fitness experience.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user sets a desired alarm time and the required number of dumbbell repetitions.

  2. 02

    The device triggers an audible alarm at the scheduled time.

  3. 03

    The user performs dumbbell movements to complete the required repetitions.

What it is

The device allows users to configure an alarm time and set a target number of dumbbell repetitions required to deactivate the alarm. When the scheduled time is reached, the system activates an audible alert and monitors the user's dumbbell movements through an integrated motion-sensing mechanism

Key features

Alarm-based morning fitness motivation

Movement detection and real-time repetition tracking

Interactive alarm deactivation through physical activity

Dumbbell-inspired design with a comfortable grip

Where it's used

Fitness enthusiasts Student dorm rooms Home workouts Personal fitness routines Individuals seeking to overcome snoozing habits Develop a more active Disciplined morning routine

Benefits

  • Encourages users to begin their day with physical activity
  • Helps reduce habitual snoozing and oversleeping
  • Promotes consistency in daily fitness routines
  • Combines time management with physical exercise
  • Makes waking up more engaging, interactive, and motivating
