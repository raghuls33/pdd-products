Pet Feeder

A smart automatic pet feeder designed to provide convenient and reliable feeding for pets.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The pet owner connects the device to a smartphone for remote monitoring and control.

  2. 02

    The built-in camera allows the owner to observe the pet in real time.

  3. 03

    Food is automatically dispensed in measured portions when the pet approaches.

What it is

A smart pet feeding device allows pet owners to monitor their pets remotely using a smartphone and dispense measured portions of food automatically.

Key features

Automatic food dispensing with measured portions

Remote monitoring and control through a smartphone

Built-in camera for real-time pet observation

Where it's used

Homes Busy pet owners Animal care centers Pet boarding facilities Other environments where pets require regular feeding Remote supervision

Benefits

  • Ensures pets receive food at scheduled times
  • Helps prevent overfeeding through portion control
  • Allows pet owners to monitor their pets while away
  • Reduces the need for manual feeding
  • Supports consistent feeding habits and daily pet care
  • Provides greater convenience and peace of mind for pet owners
