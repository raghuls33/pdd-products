Digital Sand Clock

A digital hourglass timer designed to visualize the passing of time through a flowing light display.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user activates the device using a simple push-button control.

  2. 02

    The illuminated display begins to flow from the top section toward the bottom section, simulating the movement of sand in a traditional hourglass.

  3. 03

    As time passes, the light display gradually shifts to indicate the elapsed time and the time remaining.

What it is

The device recreates the experience of a traditional hourglass, with illuminated “sand” moving from the top section to the bottom section to indicate elapsed time and remaining duration.

Key features

Flowing LED display that visually represents the passage of time

Simple push-button operation for convenient starting and resetting

Compact and decorative enclosure suitable for desks and home interiors

Supports focused activities and structured time management
