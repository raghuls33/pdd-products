Running Alarm Clock

The Running Alarm Clock is an interactive alarm device designed to encourage users to wake up on time by moving away when the alarm rings. It aims to improve punctuality and reduce the habit of repeatedly snoozing alarms through a mobile robotic mechanism, motion control, and an audible alarm system.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The alarm is set to the desired wake-up time.

  2. 02

    When the scheduled time arrives, the alarm sounds and the clock begins moving.

  3. 03

    The user must get out of bed and catch or stop the moving clock to turn off the alarm.

What it is

A Running Alarm Clock is an innovative alarm device designed to encourage users to wake up and get out of bed by moving away when the alarm sounds. It uses a motorized mechanism to move across a surface, prompting the user to physically get up and turn off the alarm.

Key features

Programmable alarm with audible alerts

Motorized movement to encourage physical activity

Alarm control buttons and rechargeable or battery-powered operation

Where it's used

Students and working professionals People who struggle to wake up on time Personal time-management and wake-up routines

Benefits

  • Encourages users to get out of bed to stop the alarm
  • Promotes a more active waking-up routine
  • Combines timekeeping with an interactive robotic mechanism
