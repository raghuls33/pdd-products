Math Gobbler

An interactive, voice-driven educational kiosk designed to make mathematics fun, engaging, and rewarding for learners.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The device displays a mathematical question and presents it through audio.

  2. 02

    The user listens to the question and responds verbally through the microphone.

  3. 03

    The system captures and processes the user's voice response to determine the answer.

  4. 04

    When the answer is correct, the device automatically dispenses candy as a reward, encouraging continued participation and learning.

What it is

The device presents mathematical questions through visual displays and audio, allowing users to answer verbally to get candy as reward.

Key features

Interactive display and audio-based question presentation

Voice-controlled answer submission

Automatic answer evaluation

Automatic candy dispensing for correct answers

Where it's used

Schools STEM learning centers Educational exhibitions Children's science museums Interactive activity zones Educational events

Benefits

  • Makes mathematics more fun and engaging for learners
  • Encourages active participation through interactive challenges
  • Promotes learning through rewards and positive reinforcement
  • Helps improve interest in mathematical problem-solving
  • Creates an enjoyable and memorable educational experience
