Spell Bot

An interactive spelling learning device designed to improve English vocabulary, pronunciation, and spelling accuracy through audio-based learning and on-screen interaction.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The device pronounces a word through audio prompts.

  2. 02

    The user listens carefully and enters the spelling using the touchscreen.

  3. 03

    The system checks the entered spelling against the correct answer.

  4. 04

    Instant audio-visual feedback indicates whether the answer is correct or incorrect.

What it is

The device presents words through audio prompts, allowing users to listen to the pronunciation and enter the corresponding spelling using the touchscreen interface. The system evaluates the entered response against the correct spelling and provides immediate audio-visual feedback to indicate whether the answer is correct or incorrect.

Key features

Audio-based word pronunciation and voice prompts

Touchscreen input for entering spellings

Instant audio-visual feedback on answers

Where it's used

Primary schools, language learning centers, phonics coaching institutes, activity centers, early education programs, and home-based learning environments to develop English vocabulary, pronunciation, phonics awareness, and spelling confidence.

Benefits

  • Improves English spelling accuracy and vocabulary retention
  • Enhances listening skills and word recognition
  • Supports pronunciation and phonics development
  • Encourages independent learning through interactive self-assessment
  • Builds confidence through progressive difficulty levels
  • Makes language learning more engaging and enjoyable
