Portable ECG Monitor

A portable monitor that measures heart rate and shows real-time ECG waveforms on a display.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user interacts with the device and places the appropriate sensors for measurement.

  2. 02

    The heart rate sensor detects the user's heartbeat.

  3. 03

    The ECG sensor captures electrical signals generated by the heart.

  4. 04

    The device processes the collected signals and displays the heart rate and ECG waveforms on the screen.

What it is

A Vital Signs Monitoring Device designed to measure and display heart rate and electrocardiogram (ECG) signals. It helps users understand basic heart rate patterns and the electrical activity of the heart.

Key features

Heart rate monitoring

ECG waveform visualization

Real-time display of heart activity

User-friendly interface

Portable, battery-powered design

Compact 3D-printed enclosure
