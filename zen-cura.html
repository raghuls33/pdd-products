Zen Cura

Zen Cura is a chitosan-based bandage designed to support wound care through a biomaterial-based approach.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The affected area is cleaned and dried before application.

  2. 02

    The bandage is placed over the intended area according to the product's instructions.

  3. 03

    The dressing provides a protective covering over the application site.

  4. 04

    The chitosan-based material is intended to support wound-care functions, depending on its formulation and validated performance.

What it is

It is presented as a wound dressing with potential antimicrobial and wound-healing properties, intended for application to clean, dry skin. The product explores the use of chitosan, a naturally derived biopolymer, in the development of advanced wound-care materials.

Key features

Chitosan-based bandage formulation

Designed for wound-care applications

Intended to provide a protective wound covering

Explores antimicrobial and wound-healing properties.

Where it's used

Wound-care research, biomaterial development, dressing formulation studies, biomedical engineering projects, and educational demonstrations involving chitosan-based healthcare materials.

Benefits

  • Supports research into alternative wound-dressing materials
  • Explores the potential use of chitosan in wound care
  • Provides a convenient bandage format for topical application
  • Encourages development of advanced biomaterial-based dressings
  • Offers a platform for studying protective wound-care materials
