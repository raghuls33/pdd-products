Braille Audio Assistive Device

The Braille Audio Assistive Device is a self-learning tool that teaches visually impaired children Braille letters and numbers through touch input and audio feedback.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The child forms a Braille character using the touch-based input.

  2. 02

    The device recognises the pattern.

  3. 03

    It plays the corresponding letter or number aloud.

  4. 04

    The child confirms what they entered and learns by repetition.

What it is

An interactive Braille trainer. The child enters a Braille pattern by touch, and the device speaks the matching letter or number aloud.

Key features

Touch-based Braille input

Audio playback of letters and numbers

Designed for independent, self-paced learning

Where it's used

Special education centres Schools for visually impaired children Home-based learning

Benefits

  • Supports Braille literacy from an early age
  • Builds alphabet and number recognition
  • Lets children practise independently without a teacher presence
