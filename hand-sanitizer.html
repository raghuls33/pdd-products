Hand Sanitizer

A touchless hand sanitizing system designed to detect a user's hands automatically and dispense a fine disinfectant mist for effective hand sanitization.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places their hands near the device.

  2. 02

    The ultrasonic sensor detects the presence of the hands.

  3. 03

    The system automatically activates the mist generation mechanism.

  4. 04

    A fine disinfectant mist is dispersed with the help of an airflow system to provide convenient hand coverage.

What it is

The Touchless Hand Sanitizing System uses an ultrasonic sensor to detect hands and automatically dispense disinfectant mist without physical contact. It provides convenient, hygienic sanitization while reducing cross-contamination and minimizing disinfectant wastage.

Key features

Touch-free hand detection and sanitization

Automatic disinfectant mist dispensing

Fine mist distribution for hand coverage

Hygienic operation with reduced cross-contamination

Efficient use of disinfectant with minimal wastage

Where it's used

Hospitals, clinics, educational institutions, corporate offices, public entryways, commercial complexes, healthcare facilities, and other high-traffic areas where maintaining hand hygiene is essential.

Benefits

  • Promotes safe and hygienic hand sanitization
  • Reduces the risk of cross-contamination by eliminating physical contact
  • Provides quick and convenient disinfection
  • Helps maintain hygiene in public and shared spaces
  • Minimizes disinfectant wastage through controlled dispensing
  • Supports infection prevention and regular hand hygiene practices
