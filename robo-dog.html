Robo Dog

A smart robotic guide dog designed to assist visually impaired individuals in navigating their surroundings safely and independently.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user walks with the robotic guide dog as it moves along their path.

  2. 02

    The device detects obstacles ahead and identifies potential obstructions.

  3. 03

    When an object is detected, it produces a barking sound to alert the user.

What it is

A robot dog which detects obstacles in the user's path and alerts them through barking sounds. The device can also recognize known and unknown people nearby and provide spoken descriptions of the surroundings, helping users move around with greater confidence and awareness.

Key features

Automatic obstacle detection and alert system

Barking sounds to warn users about objects in their path

Robotic movement to support guided walking

Where it's used

Indoor spaces, hallways, assisted living homes, rehabilitation centers, public buildings, and everyday walking environments where visually impaired individuals require assistance with navigation and obstacle awareness.

Benefits

  • Helps visually impaired individuals navigate their surroundings more safely
  • Reduces the risk of bumping into obstacles while walking
  • Provides audio alerts for timely obstacle awareness
  • Improves awareness of nearby people and surrounding environments
  • Supports greater independence and confidence during daily activities
