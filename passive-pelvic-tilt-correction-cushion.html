Passive Pelvic Tilt Correction Cushion

This posture-support cushion uses dynamic air-chamber pressure redistribution to help correct pelvic tilt, improve sitting alignment and reduce discomfort.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The cushion is placed on a chair or wheelchair seat.

  2. 02

    As the user sits, the air chambers redistribute pressure dynamically.

  3. 03

    This passive correction nudges the pelvis out of a tilted position and supports a healthier spinal alignment.

What it is

A seat cushion with internal air chambers. Instead of a fixed foam shape, the chambers shift pressure under the user to guide the pelvis toward a neutral, upright position.

Key features

Dynamic air-chamber pressure redistribution

Passive correction with no effort needed from the user

Fits standard office and therapy seating

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Office seating Anyone who sits for long periods

Benefits

  • Helps correct pelvic tilt and improve sitting posture
  • Supports proper pelvic and spinal alignment
  • Reduces discomfort from prolonged sitting
