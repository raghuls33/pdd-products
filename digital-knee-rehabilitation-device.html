Rehab & assistive · Product design

Digital Knee Rehabilitation Device

The Digital Knee Rehabilitation Device tracks knee-exercise repetitions, lets targets be set from a mobile interface, and sounds a buzzer when the target is reached.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The therapist or patient sets an exercise target in the mobile interface.

  2. 02

    The patient performs the prescribed knee movements.

  3. 03

    The device counts each repetition.

  4. 04

    A buzzer sounds when the target is reached.

What it is

A knee exercise tracker for structured rehab. It counts each prescribed knee movement and tells the patient when they have done enough.

Key features

Automatic repetition tracking

Exercise targets set through a mobile interface

Buzzer alert on reaching the target

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Knee rehabilitation Post-injury Post-surgery recovery Mobility training

Benefits

  • Helps patients complete their full prescribed routine
  • Gives structure to home exercise between clinic visits
  • Lets therapists monitor exercise progress
