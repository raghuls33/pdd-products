Portable Shuttlecock Thrower

The Automated Badminton Shuttlecock Thrower is a portable, battery-powered training device designed to assist badminton players in improving their skills through automated shuttlecock delivery.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user loads shuttlecocks into the device's feeding mechanism.

  2. 02

    The device launches shuttlecocks according to pre-programmed sequences or commands provided through the mobile application.

  3. 03

    The device moves in different directions to deliver shuttlecocks across various areas of the court.

What it is

A portable, battery-powered badminton shuttlecock thrower designed to deliver shuttlecocks automatically or manually for convenient and effective training by controlled motor movements.

Key features

Automatic shuttlecock launching modes

Bluetooth-enabled mobile application control

Pre-programmed launching sequences for structured practice

Four-directional mobility for dynamic shuttlecock delivery

Portable, battery-powered design for convenient use

Where it's used

Badminton training academies, school sports clubs, sports coaching centers, fitness centers, and individual players practicing independently to improve footwork, reaction speed, shot consistency, and overall badminton performance.

Benefits

  • Enables independent badminton practice without a manual shuttlecock feeder
  • Improves footwork, agility, and reaction speed through varied shuttlecock delivery
  • Supports consistent shot practice and skill development
  • Provides flexible training through automatic sequences and real-time control
  • Enhances training efficiency with dynamic, multi-directional movement
  • Offers a convenient and portable solution for badminton practice in different training environments
