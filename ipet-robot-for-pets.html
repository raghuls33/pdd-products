iPet Robot for Pets

A mobile pet companion robot that moves around on its own, detects a ball, and launches it.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The robot is switched on and can be driven from a mobile phone over Bluetooth, or left to run in autonomous mode.

  2. 02

    In autonomous mode, it moves around and stops when it senses an obstacle ahead.

  3. 03

    When a ball is detected, the launching mechanism holds it and releases it.

  4. 04

    The ball is thrown for the pet to chase, and the robot starts its motion sequence again.

What it is

With iPet, dogs can play fetch without a person throwing. It keeps pets active and entertained, and lets wheelchair users and busy owners enjoy regular playtime with their pets.

Key features

Automatic ball detection and launching

Two control modes: mobile manual control and autonomous play

Obstacle detection for safe indoor movement

Rechargeable battery for cordless operation

Where it's used

Homes Apartments Pet care centers Dog training centers Veterinary clinics Assistive living for wheelchair users Persons with limited mobility STEM Robotics demonstrations

Benefits

  • Keeps pets active, healthy, and free from boredom
  • Reduces destructive behaviour in dogs left alone
  • Lets wheelchair users and busy owners play with their pets easily
  • Strengthens the bond between pets and owners
  • A more affordable alternative to imported automatic ball launchers
