Robo Luggage Carrier

An AI-powered smart luggage system designed to automatically detect, track, and follow its user in real time.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The system detects and identifies the user using an intelligent vision-based tracking mechanism.

  2. 02

    It continuously tracks the user's position and movement in real time.

  3. 03

    The system processes the tracking information and controls the luggage's movement accordingly.

  4. 04

    The luggage autonomously follows the user, providing smooth and convenient transportation of personal belongings.

What it is

The system identifies the user, continuously monitors their position and movement, and processes the tracking information to determine the appropriate direction of travel. Based on the detected movement, an integrated motor control system guides the luggage along the user's path, enabling hands-free transportation of personal belongings.

Key features

AI-powered user detection and tracking

Autonomous movement and user-following capability

Real-time vision-based tracking

Intelligent motor control for luggage navigation

Where it's used

Airports, railway stations, bus terminals, shopping complexes, transit hubs, hotels, and other public spaces where travelers need convenient assistance in transporting their luggage.

Benefits

  • Reduces the physical effort required to carry heavy luggage
  • Provides a hands-free and convenient travel experience
  • Improves mobility and ease of movement in crowded transit areas
  • Minimizes the need for manually pulling or pushing luggage
  • Enhances travel convenience through autonomous operation
  • Supports smarter and more efficient luggage transportation
