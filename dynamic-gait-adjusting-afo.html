Dynamic Gait Adjusting AFO

The Dynamic Gait Adjusting AFO is an assistive ankle-foot orthosis that uses gyroscope feedback to monitor leg movement and adjust its support as the patient walks.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The patient wears the AFO on the affected leg.

  2. 02

    A gyroscope tracks leg movement and orientation during walking.

  3. 03

    The device adjusts its support dynamically based on that feedback.

  4. 04

    The result is steadier steps and a better walking posture.

What it is

An ankle-foot orthosis (AFO) fitted with a gyroscope. Unlike a rigid brace, it responds to how the leg is actually moving and changes the support it gives through the gait cycle.

Key features

Gyroscope-based leg-movement monitoring

Dynamic, adaptive ankle support

Wearable design for everyday walking and gait training

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Gait training Mobility support for patients who need better balance Walking assistance

Benefits

  • Improves gait stability and walking posture
  • Adapts to the patient rather than holding the ankle in one fixed position
  • Supports patients as they rebuild balance and confidence
