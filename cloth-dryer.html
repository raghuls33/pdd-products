Cloth Dryer

A compact and efficient cloth dryer designed to dry clothes in just 25–30 minutes in a controlled environment.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user places clothes inside the dryer and arranges them on the hanging rails.

  2. 02

    The dryer circulates warm air throughout the enclosed chamber to accelerate moisture removal.

  3. 03

    The drying process operates in a controlled environment to support effective and gentle fabric drying.

  4. 04

    The user can monitor the drying cycle through the display for a simple and convenient experience.

What it is

The device provides a dedicated drying chamber in which clothes are arranged on hanging rails, allowing warm air to circulate around the garments for effective and uniform drying. The enclosed environment helps protect clothes from dust and external contaminants while reducing dependence on natural sunlight and outdoor drying conditions.

Key features

Fast and efficient clothes drying in 25–30 minutes

Enclosed warm-air circulation for uniform drying

Helps protect clothes from dust and external exposure
