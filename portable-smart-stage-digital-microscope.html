Portable Smart Stage Digital Microscope

This compact digital microscope has an adjustable stage for precise sample positioning, with real-time viewing and image capture in a portable body.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    A sample is placed on the adjustable stage.

  2. 02

    The stage is moved to position the area of interest precisely.

  3. 03

    The image is viewed live on a display.

  4. 04

    Users capture images for records, reports or teaching.

What it is

A digital microscope that shows the magnified sample on a screen rather than through an eyepiece. Its adjustable stage lets users position slides precisely, and it is small enough to carry to the field.

Key features

Adjustable smart stage for precise positioning

Real-time digital viewing

Image capture for documentation

Compact and portable

Schools Colleges Laboratories Research work Field-based observation

Benefits

  • Makes microscopy easier to share: several people can view one screen
  • Builds a digital record of samples
  • Takes microscopic analysis out of the lab and into the field
