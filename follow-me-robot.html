Follow Me Robot

The Follow Me Robot is a mobile robot designed to follow a person or a target while moving. It can assist with carrying lightweight items or demonstrate autonomous navigation using sensors and a motor-control system.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The sensing system detects the person or target in front of the robot.

  2. 02

    The controller estimates the target's direction or distance from sensor readings.

  3. 03

    The motor driver adjusts the wheels so the robot follows while attempting to maintain a suitable distance.

What it is

A robot that detects and tracks a person or target using a suitable sensor or camera system, then controls its motors to maintain movement behind the target.

Key features

Target-detection and tracking system

Controller-based speed and direction control

Motorized movement

Where it's used

Educational robotics Indoor assistance prototypes Laboratories Demonstrations Lightweight item transport in controlled environments

Benefits

  • Reduces the need to steer the robot manually
  • Demonstrates sensor-based tracking and motor control
  • Can support hands-free movement of lightweight items
  • Provides a platform for learning autonomous robotics
