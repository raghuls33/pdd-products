Healvanta Biodegradable Pain Patch

A Biodegradable Pain Relief Patch designed to provide a convenient, topical application for pain management.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The patch is placed on the intended area of the skin according to the product's instructions.

  2. 02

    The patch forms a layer of contact with the skin at the application site.

  3. 03

    The formulation's ingredients interact with the skin surface as intended by the product design.

  4. 04

    The patch is removed and disposed of according to the manufacturer's instructions.

What it is

The patch incorporates materials such as chitosan, lactic acid, zinc sulphide, sodium chloride, ethanol, and distilled water in its formulation. Its design explores the use of biodegradable materials in topical patch development, combining ease of application with material-based approaches to healthcare.
