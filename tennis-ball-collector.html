Tennis Ball Collector

An autonomous tennis ball collection robot designed to detect, track, and retrieve tennis balls scattered across the court.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The device visually scans the tennis court to detect and locate scattered tennis balls.

  2. 02

    It tracks the balls and navigates toward them autonomously.

  3. 03

    The motorized intake mechanism sweeps up and collects the tennis balls.

What it is

The device uses vision-based object detection to identify the positions of tennis balls and navigate toward them with minimal human intervention. Once a ball is detected, the robot moves toward its location and activates a motorized intake mechanism to collect it.

Key features

Autonomous tennis ball detection and tracking

Vision-guided navigation across the court

Motorized ball sweeping and collection mechanism

Reduces manual effort and simplifies ball collection

Where it's used

Tennis academies Sports complexes Training centers School tennis courts Coaching facilities Individual practice sessions

Benefits

  • Saves time by automating tennis ball collection
  • Improves training efficiency by reducing interruptions
  • Reduces physical strain on coaches and players
  • Enables convenient collection of scattered tennis balls
  • Supports smoother and more productive practice sessions
