Chat Bot

A smart, interactive, voice-enabled AI assistant designed to provide real-time conversational support.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user interacts with the device by speaking a question or command.

  2. 02

    The device captures and processes the user's voice input.

  3. 03

    The AI assistant interprets the request and generates a relevant response.

  4. 04

    The response is delivered through audio output and displayed on the screen for easy reference.

What it is

The device captures user speech, processes the spoken input, and interprets the request to generate relevant responses using artificial intelligence. The generated responses are delivered through audio output and presented on an integrated display, enabling users to both hear and view the information provided.

Key features

Voice-based interaction for hands-free operation

Real-time conversational assistance

AI-powered responses to user queries

Audio responses for natural communication

Visual interface for displaying information

Where it's used

Office front desks Educational institutions Interactive help desks Customer assistance kiosks Reception areas Smart home automation systems

Benefits

  • Provides quick and convenient access to information
  • Enables natural, hands-free interaction
  • Enhances user engagement through voice and visual communication
  • Reduces the need for manual typing and navigation
  • Supports customer service and everyday task assistance
  • Improves accessibility and convenience in interactive environments
