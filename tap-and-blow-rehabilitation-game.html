Tap and Blow Rehabilitation Game

Tap and Blow is an interactive breathing-exercise game with a dino-themed interface that responds to the patient's blowing effort.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The patient blows into or toward the sensor.

  2. 02

    The device measures the blowing effort.

  3. 03

    The dino character responds in the game according to that effort.

  4. 04

    The patient keeps practising controlled breaths to progress.

What it is

A breath-controlled game. A sensor picks up how hard the patient blows, and the on-screen dino reacts, turning breathing exercises into play.

Key features

Breath-effort sensing

Fun dino-themed game interface

Rewards controlled, repeated breathing

Where it's used

Respiratory physiotherapy Breathing exercises Pulmonary rehabilitation General patient therapy

Benefits

  • Encourages controlled breathing practice
  • Makes repetitive breathing exercises engaging, especially for children
  • Improves participation in rehabilitation sessions
