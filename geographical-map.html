Geographical Map

An interactive geography learning device designed to help users explore geographical regions through touch-based interaction and illuminated visual feedback.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user touches a selected state on the map.

  2. 02

    The device detects the touch and identifies the selected state.

  3. 03

    The corresponding state and its geographical boundaries illuminate with vibrant colors.

  4. 04

    The visual feedback helps users identify and understand the location and boundaries of the selected state.

What it is

The device allows users to select individual states directly on a map, where the touch input is detected and processed to identify the selected geographical region. The corresponding state and its boundaries are then illuminated with vibrant colors, providing a clear visual representation of its location and shape.

Key features

Interactive touch-based state selection

Illuminated state boundaries for clear geographical identification

Vibrant lighting for enhanced visual learning

Clear demarcation of individual states

Where it's used

School social studies classrooms Geography laboratories STEM exhibition spaces Children's science centers Educational resource libraries Interactive learning environments

Benefits

  • Makes geography learning more interactive and engaging
  • Helps students recognize states and understand their geographical boundaries
  • Encourages hands-on exploration and active learning
  • Improves visual understanding of geographical locations
  • Supports collaborative learning and classroom demonstrations
  • Enhances curiosity and interest in geography through intuitive visual feedback
