3D Scanner

A 3D scanning device designed to capture the shape and size of physical objects and convert them into digital 3D models in STL format.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user places the physical object on the scanning platform.

  2. 02

    The device scans the object to capture its shape and dimensions from different positions.

  3. 03

    The collected data is processed to generate a digital 3D representation of the object.

  4. 04

    The resulting 3D model is saved in STL format for further design, analysis, or 3D printing.

What it is

The device provides a fast and convenient way to digitize real-world objects for design, development, and reverse engineering without the need for manual measurements.

Key features

Automated object scanning and dimension capture

Three-dimensional shape visualization

Digital 3D model generation in STL format

Convenient design for scanning and prototyping applications

Where it's used

Makerspaces Engineering laboratories Rapid prototyping studios Dental Medical modelling Product development facilities Educational institutions Reverse engineering workshops

Benefits

  • Enables quick conversion of physical objects into digital 3D models
  • Reduces the need for manual measurements and modelling.
  • Supports product design, development, and reverse engineering
  • Facilitates rapid prototyping and 3D printing workflows
  • Improves convenience and efficiency in physical-to-digital model creation
