Eco-Wrap

Eco-Wrap is a biodegradable food wrap made from sugarcane bagasse cellulose, offering a practical wrapping solution for everyday food storage.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The wrap film is placed over a food item or container.

  2. 02

    It forms a protective covering that helps reduce direct exposure to dust and external contaminants.

  3. 03

    The covering helps maintain food freshness during short-term storage.

What it is

Eco-Wrap is a biodegradable food wrap film made from sugarcane bagasse cellulose, designed as an alternative to conventional plastic food wraps. It helps cover and protect food items while supporting freshness preservation and environmentally conscious food packaging

Key features

Sugarcane bagasse cellulose-based formulation

Designed as a biodegradable food wrap film

Suitable for covering food items and containers

Helps protect food during storage

Where it's used

Households Restaurants Bakeries Catering services Food packaging facilities Grocery stores Other food-service environments requiring wrapping Covering solutions for food items

Benefits

  • Reduces reliance on conventional plastic food wraps
  • Makes use of sugarcane bagasse as a plant-derived raw material
  • Supports more environmentally conscious food packaging practices
  • Helps cover and protect food during storage
  • Offers a convenient solution for everyday food wrapping
  • Encourages the development of biodegradable packaging alternatives
