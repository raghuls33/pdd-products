Pill Box

An automated medicine dispenser designed to remind users and dispense tablets at their prescribed times

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user sets or follows a prescribed medication schedule.

  2. 02

    The device tracks the scheduled medication times automatically.

  3. 03

    At the prescribed time, it provides visual alerts and clear voice announcements to remind the user.

  4. 04

    The device automatically dispenses the scheduled tablets from the designated compartment.

What it is

It helps individuals follow their medication schedules by providing timely alerts, voice announcements, and automatic tablet dispensing.

Key features

Automatic, schedule-based medicine dispensing

Timely medication reminders through voice announcements

Visual alerts and on-screen information

Organized tablet dispensing from multiple compartments

Supports consistent medication routines

Where it's used

Ideal for elderly care at home, assisted living facilities, memory care centers, hospitals, and individuals managing multiple prescriptions or busy daily schedules.

Benefits

  • Helps reduce missed or delayed medication doses
  • Supports a regular and organized medication routine
  • Reduces the need to remember every medication time manually
  • Simplifies medication management for elderly individuals and caregivers
  • Helps minimize dispensing errors when properly configured
  • Promotes greater convenience and independence in daily healthcare management
