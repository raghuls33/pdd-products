Aero Air Quality Monitor

Aero is a simple device that shows the condition of the air in a room using happy and sad faces. It also displays the room temperature, humidity and air condition when touched.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The device checks the condition of the air in the room.

  2. 02

    It shows a happy face when the air is good and a sad face when the air is poor.

  3. 03

    When touched, the screen shows the room temperature, humidity and air condition.

What it is

A small device that helps people understand the air around them. It shows a happy face when the air is good and a sad face when the air is poor. When touched, it displays the room temperature, humidity and air condition.

Key features

Happy and sad faces to show the air condition

Touch to view room details

Shows room temperature and humidity

Where it's used

Homes Classrooms Offices Other indoor spaces

Benefits

  • Helps people understand the air around them
  • Gives a simple visual sign of the air condition
  • Makes it easy to check room temperature and humidity
