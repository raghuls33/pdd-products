Resistance Band with Visual Load Indicator

This smart resistance band measures the force applied during exercise and shows it in real time, so users can hold the right resistance and intensity.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user performs an exercise with the band.

  2. 02

    A sensor measures the applied force.

  3. 03

    A visual indicator shows the load live.

  4. 04

    The user adjusts effort to stay at the target resistance.

What it is

A resistance band with a built-in force sensor and visual display. It turns a simple elastic band into a measured training tool.

Key features

Real-time force measurement

Visual load indicator

Portable band format

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Fitness training Strength conditioning

Benefits

  • Keeps exercise at the intended intensity, not too light or too heavy
  • Gives therapists an objective measure of patient effort
  • Supports proper technique and safer progression
