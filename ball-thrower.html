Ball Thrower

An automatic ball launching machine designed to launch balls for consistent and convenient practice.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user loads multiple balls into the machine's storage compartment.

  2. 02

    The machine feeds the balls automatically into the launching mechanism.

  3. 03

    The balls are launched at adjustable speeds and distances to suit different practice needs.

What it is

A ball thrower, can hold multiple balls at once and launch them automatically, allowing users to enjoy continuous training and play without requiring another person to throw the ball.

Key features

Automatic ball feeding and launching

Multiple-ball storage for continuous operation

Consistent ball delivery for repetitive practice

Where it's used

Sports training sessions Backyard play Cricket Tennis practice drills Recreational activities Interactive play for keeping active pets engaged Entertained

Benefits

  • Enables independent practice without requiring another person to throw the ball
  • Provides consistent ball delivery for repetitive training drills
  • Supports the development of sports skills, coordination, and reaction time
  • Offers an engaging and convenient way to stay active and enjoy recreational play
