IV Pole

The Smart Hospital IV Stand is an intelligent medical monitoring system designed to track intravenous (IV) fluid consumption and estimate the remaining fluid level in real time.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The IV fluid bag is attached to the smart monitoring stand.

  2. 02

    The device continuously monitors changes in the fluid bag's weight as the fluid is consumed.

  3. 03

    It processes the weight measurements to track fluid usage and estimate the remaining fluid level.

  4. 04

    The information is displayed on the onboard screen

What it is

The device uses weight-based sensing to measure changes in the mass of an attached IV fluid bag as the fluid is administered to the patient. The collected measurements are processed to monitor fluid usage and determine the estimated quantity of fluid remaining.

Key features

Continuous monitoring of IV fluid bag weight

Real-time tracking of fluid consumption

Instant display of weight information

Reduces the need for frequent manual IV level checks

Where it's used

Hospital intensive care units (ICUs), general recovery wards, emergency rooms, nursing stations, outpatient clinics, and other healthcare facilities requiring continuous IV fluid monitoring and efficient saline bottle management.

Benefits

  • Helps prevent IV fluid depletion and dry-line hazards through timely monitoring
  • Reduces the need for nurses to physically inspect IV fluid levels frequently
  • Supports efficient management of IV fluid consumption
  • Enables remote monitoring and improves access to fluid status information
  • Helps healthcare staff respond promptly when fluid levels become low
  • Improves nursing workflow and supports safer, more effective patient care
