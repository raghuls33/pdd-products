Rehab Therapeutic Belt

The Rehab Therapeutic Belt is a rechargeable, battery-powered belt that delivers adjustable electrical stimulation to support muscle activation, strengthening and rehabilitation.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The belt is worn over the target muscle area.

  2. 02

    The therapist selects a stimulation level suited to the patient.

  3. 03

    Electrical stimulation activates the muscles in a controlled way.

What it is

A wearable stimulation belt. Built-in electrodes deliver electrical stimulation to the muscles underneath, and the rechargeable battery means no cables during use.

Key features

Adjustable electrical stimulation

Rechargeable battery for cordless use

Portable, wearable belt design

Where it's used

Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Muscle strengthening Recovery Therapeutic exercise

Benefits

  • Supports controlled muscle activation, including in weakened muscles
  • Adjustable settings allow personalised treatment
  • Convenient to use during therapy sessions without being tethered
