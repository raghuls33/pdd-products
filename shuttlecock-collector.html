Shuttlecock Collector

The Shuttle Cock Collector is an autonomous and remote-controlled robotic system designed to efficiently collect badminton shuttlecocks from the court floor, saving time and manual effort during practice sessions and tournaments.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    Dual controllers (Raspberry Pi and Arduino) drive the DC gear motors to move the vehicle across the court floor.

  2. 02

    Front-mounted rotating rollers actively sweep scattered shuttlecocks off the floor surface.

  3. 03

    The picked shuttlecocks are guided upward along the ramp and deposited into the rear storage compartment.

What it is

A smart, motorized mobile collector device featuring a front rotating roller and an expansive storage bed. Driven by Raspberry Pi and Arduino Uno, it traverses badminton courts to gather shuttlecocks automatically or via remote control.

Key features

Dual control: autonomous sweep & remote control

Active front rotating roller brush sweep mechanism

Integrated Raspberry Pi & Arduino Uno dual brains

High-capacity storage bed for 20–30 shuttlecocks

Where it's used

Indoor badminton courts Training centers Badminton academies School and college sports facilities

Benefits

  • Drastically reduces manual bending and physical effort
  • Minimizes transition downtime between training drills
  • Safe, non-slip rubber wheels and protective enclosure
  • Compact, portable, and user-friendly operation
  • Cordless 12V DC power for seamless mobility across courts
