Nicotine-Based Insecticide

A Nicotine-Based Insecticide is a plant-derived pest-control formulation.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The insecticide is applied directly to the target insect according to the product instructions.

  2. 02

    The active nicotine-based formulation affects the insect's nervous system.

  3. 03

    The affected insect may become immobilized after exposure.

What it is

The insecticide is developed to target insects through direct application, with the formulation intended to immobilize affected insects. The product combines plant-derived nicotine with other formulation ingredients to provide an alternative approach to conventional insect-control products.

Key features

Plant-derived insecticide formulation

Extracted from Nicotiana tabacum

Designed for direct application to insects

Intended to immobilize targeted pests

Where it's used

Agricultural pest-management research, botanical insecticide studies, plant-protection research, and educational demonstrations involving plant-derived pest-control formulations.

Benefits

  • Explores plant-derived approaches to insect control
  • Provides a formulation for studying botanical insecticides
  • Supports research into alternative pest-management methods
  • Offers a practical example of plant-based active compounds in pest-control development
