Smart Tissue and Toothpick Dispenser

An automatic, touch-free tissue and toothpick dispenser designed to provide convenient and hygienic access to everyday dining essentials.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user brings their hand near the dispenser.

  2. 02

    The device automatically detects the presence of the hand.

  3. 03

    It dispenses a tissue or toothpick as needed without requiring the user to touch the device.

What it is

A tooth pick and tissue dispensing device detects the user's hand and dispenses tissues or toothpicks without requiring physical contact with the device, helping maintain cleanliness and reduce the spread of germs.

Key features

Touch-free dispensing of tissues and toothpicks

Automatic hand detection, Convenient and hygienic operation

User-friendly design for everyday use

Where it's used

Dining tables Restaurants Hotels Office pantries Homes Cafeterias Other food-serving environments where hygiene Convenience are important

Benefits

  • Minimizes direct contact with the dispenser, helping reduce the spread of germs
  • Promotes better hygiene in dining environments
  • Provides quick and convenient access to tissues and toothpicks
  • Enhances the overall dining experience
  • Reduces the need for manual dispensing
  • Supports a cleaner, safer, and more comfortable environment
