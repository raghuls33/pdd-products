Soil Nutrient Analyser

The Portable Soil Health Testing Device is a field-based agricultural monitoring system designed to assess essential soil nutrients, chemical properties, moisture levels, and temperature.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user inserts the device's probe into the soil to collect soil condition measurements.

  2. 02

    The device detects essential soil nutrients and evaluates key chemical properties and moisture levels.

  3. 03

    The system processes the sensor readings to determine the soil's health and fertility status.

  4. 04

    The measured values are displayed on the screen

What it is

The device uses an integrated probe and sensing system, the device collects soil measurements and processes the readings to provide immediate information about soil health and fertility.

Key features

Direct soil testing for essential macronutrients

Real-time monitoring of soil chemical properties

Soil moisture and temperature assessment

Instant display of soil health information.

Where it's used

Precision farming Agricultural research institutes Polyhouse operations Fertilizer advisory centers Organic farms Agricultural extension services Field-based soil health assessment programs

Benefits

  • Provides quick and convenient on-site soil health assessment
  • Helps farmers understand soil nutrient levels and fertility conditions
  • Supports balanced fertilizer application and efficient nutrient management
  • Promotes improved crop productivity and informed farming decisions
  • Reduces dependence on lengthy laboratory testing procedures
  • Encourages sustainable agricultural practices and helps prevent soil degradation
