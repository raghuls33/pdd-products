OT EnviroCare

The OT (Operation Theater) Environment Monitoring Device monitors system designed to measure and display critical ambient conditions in operating theaters.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The device continuously monitors the temperature and humidity of the surrounding environment.

  2. 02

    It detects airborne dust particles to assess air quality.

  3. 03

    The system processes the collected environmental data in real time.

  4. 04

    The measured values are displayed on the screen for easy monitoring.

What it is

The system continuously acquires temperature, humidity, and airborne dust particle data to provide real-time information about the surrounding environment.

Key features

Real-time monitoring of temperature and humidity

Continuous air quality monitoring

Instant display of environmental conditions

Visual alert indicators for abnormal conditions

Where it's used

Hospitals, operation theaters, intensive care units (ICUs), post-operative recovery rooms, neonatal care units, pharmaceutical cleanrooms, and other controlled medical environments where environmental monitoring is essential.

Benefits

  • Helps monitor critical environmental conditions in healthcare facilities
  • Supports the maintenance of controlled operating room environments
  • Provides real-time information for timely corrective action
  • Helps healthcare staff identify changes in air quality and environmental conditions
  • Supports patient safety and infection prevention efforts
  • Reduces the need for frequent manual environmental checks
