Aura Amplifier

The Aura Amplifier is a Bluetooth-enabled audio device that combines wireless music playback with dynamic, sound-responsive LED lighting.

Product prototype

How it works

4 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user connects a smartphone or other compatible device to the Aura Amplifier through Bluetooth.

  2. 02

    The device plays music wirelessly through its integrated speaker system.

  3. 03

    The audio-responsive circuit detects changes in the sound being played.

  4. 04

    The LED strip blinks and changes its lighting patterns in synchronization with the music.

What it is

The device allows users to play their favorite music wirelessly while creating an attractive visual experience through LED lights that respond to the rhythm and intensity of the sound.

Key features

Bluetooth-enabled wireless music playback

Integrated speaker system for audio output

Music-synchronized LED lighting effects

Dynamic lighting that responds to sound intensity and rhythm
