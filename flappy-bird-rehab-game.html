Rehabilitation Game – Flappy Bird

This interactive rehabilitation device turns hand exercise movements into game actions, so patients do therapy while playing a Flappy Bird–style game.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The patient performs the prescribed hand movement.

  2. 02

    The device detects the movement and converts it into a game action.

  3. 03

    The patient keeps exercising to keep the bird in the air and score points.

What it is

A hand-exercise controller linked to a simple game. Each therapeutic hand movement makes the bird fly, turning repetitive exercise into play.

Key features

Hand movement mapped directly to game control

Engaging, familiar game format

Encourages many repetitions per session

Where it's used

Hand rehabilitation Physiotherapy Motor-skill training Post-injury or post-stroke recovery

Benefits

  • Makes repetitive hand exercise motivating and fun
  • Improves hand movement and coordination
  • Increases patient participation and session length
