Interactive Human Anatomy

An interactive human anatomy learning model designed to help users explore human organs and body parts through touch.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user touches or selects a specific organ or body part on the model.

  2. 02

    The corresponding area lights up with LED illumination for easy identification.

  3. 03

    The device plays an audio explanation describing the organ's function and its role in the human body.

What it is

The device enables users to select specific organs or body parts on the model, triggering LED illumination to identify the selected region. An integrated audio system then provides relevant information about the organ's structure, function, physiological role, and associated biological facts.

Key features

Interactive touch-based organ selection

Targeted LED illumination for visual identification

Instant audio explanations of organ functions and biological facts

Hands-on and screen-free learning experience

Where it's used

School biology laboratories, science activity centers, healthcare education rooms, STEM exhibition spaces, libraries, and educational institutions for interactive human anatomy learning.

Benefits

  • Makes learning human anatomy more engaging and interactive
  • Helps users understand the location and functions of human organs
  • Combines touch, visual guidance, and audio for an enhanced learning experience
  • Encourages curiosity and hands-on exploration of biological concepts
  • Supports effective biology education for students and learners of different levels
