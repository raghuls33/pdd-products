Pitch Line Marking Robot

The Pitch Line Marking Robot is a wheeled robot that marks straight, clear lines on a pitch automatically, replacing slow and uneven manual line marking.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The robot is placed at the starting point of the line.

  2. 02

    It is switched on and moves forward on its wheels.

  3. 03

    The nozzle dispenses paint from the container as the robot moves.

  4. 04

    A continuous, even line is marked along its path.

What it is

A wheeled robot with a rigid black body, a paint container and a dispensing nozzle fitted at the front. It moves along the ground while the nozzle lays the marking line.

Key features

Automatic line marking without manual effort

Paint container with a dispensing nozzle

Rugged wheels for use on grounds

Strong enclosed body protecting the electronics

Where it's used

Cricket pitches Sports grounds Playgrounds School College fields Training grounds

Benefits

  • Gives straighter and more uniform lines
  • Saves time and labour compared with hand marking
  • Makes repeated markings quick and consistent
