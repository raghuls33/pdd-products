Mini Stepper with Pressure-Sensing Heel Cups

This compact stepper supports stepping exercises while sensing foot placement and weight distribution, giving feedback as the patient trains.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The patient places their heels in the sensing cups and steps.

  2. 02

    Sensors monitor foot placement and weight distribution.

  3. 03

    Feedback during training helps the patient correct uneven loading and control their movement.

What it is

A mini stepper fitted with pressure-sensing heel cups. It adds measurement to a familiar stepping exercise, so therapists can see how the patient loads each foot.

Key features

Pressure-sensing heel cups

Real-time feedback on foot placement and weight distribution

Compact stepper format

Where it's used

Neurorehabilitation (for example after stroke) Physiotherapy Gait training Balance improvement Mobility exercises

Benefits

  • Improves coordination and lower-limb strength
  • Helps patients even out weight bearing between legs
  • Gives therapists objective data on progress
