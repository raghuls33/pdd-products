What it is

A digital Vedic calendar and devotional companion. It shows daily Panchangam details and auspicious timings, and plays day-specific hymns and Thithi-based chants.

Key features

Digital display of daily Panchangam details and auspicious timings

Automatic playback of day-specific devotional hymns and chants

Thithi-based devotional audio for traditional observances

Where it's used

Home pooja rooms Temples Spiritual Meditation centers Retirement living spaces Wellness environments Daily devotional routines

  • Helps users follow auspicious timings for prayers and rituals
  • Encourages consistent daily spiritual practices through timely devotional audio
  • Creates a peaceful and authentic devotional atmosphere at home
  • Combines traditional Vedic calendar practices with modern smart-home convenience
  • Simplifies daily devotional routines by bringing calendar information and spiritual audio into one device
