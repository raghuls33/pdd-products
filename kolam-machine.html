Kolam Machine

An automated floor-plotting device designed to create intricate rangoli patterns, traditional kolam designs.

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The user selects a desired rangoli pattern or deity design through a mobile device.

  2. 02

    The system follows the predefined design path to move across the floor in multiple directions.

  3. 03

    The device automatically releases decorative powder along the designated path to create the selected artwork.

What it is

The device allows users to select a desired design through a mobile interface and executes the corresponding drawing path through controlled multi-directional movement.

Key features

Automated creation of intricate rangoli and kolam patterns

Precise movement for detailed geometric and deity artwork

Wireless operation through mobile device commands

Faster and more consistent floor decoration

Where it's used

Temple premises, religious festivals, wedding venues, convention halls, heritage exhibitions, cultural events, and public celebration spaces requiring traditional decorative floor artwork.

Benefits

  • Produces detailed and symmetrical floor designs with high precision
  • Reduces the time and effort required for manual rangoli drawing
  • Enables consistent powder distribution and well-defined patterns
  • Supports the creation of complex religious and geometric artwork
  • Improves efficiency when decorating large floor areas
  • Preserves traditional rangoli and kolam art through automated technology
