Vertical Aeroponics

An advanced soilless growing system that cultivates plants by suspending their roots in the air and periodically misting them with a nutrient-rich solution.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    Plants are securely seated in vertical modules with their roots suspended inside enclosed growing chambers.

  2. 02

    An automated pump and timer system delivers precise, fine mists of water and nutrients directly to the roots at regular intervals.

  3. 03

    Excess nutrient solution is captured, filtered, and recirculated to maximize resource efficiency.

What it is

An advanced soilless growing system designed to cultivate plants by suspending their roots in the air and periodically misting them with a nutrient-rich solution

Key features

Space-saving vertical tower layout for high-density indoor or outdoor farming

Automated precision misting schedules for optimal root hydration

Closed-loop water and nutrient recirculation mechanism

Where it's used

Urban farming Indoor home gardens Commercial greenhouses Educational institutions Rooftop agriculture Sustainable living initiatives

Benefits

  • Maximizes crop yield per square foot by utilizing vertical space effectively
  • Conserves significant amounts of water and nutrients compared to traditional soil farming
  • Accelerates plant growth rates through superior root oxygenation
  • Eliminates soil-borne pests and weeds for cleaner, healthier harvests
