Semi-Automatic ICU Support System

The Semi Automatic ICU Support System is a patient-handling system that helps caregivers lift, reposition, transfer and clean patients with less physical effort.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The patient is positioned on the system.

  2. 02

    The hydraulic lift raises the patient to the required height.

  3. 03

    Detachable sections give access for cleaning, repositioning or transfer.

  4. 04

    The caregiver completes the task without manually lifting the patient's full weight.

What it is

A patient-handling platform with adjustable height, a hydraulic lifting system and detachable sections. It takes the heavy lifting out of routine bedside care.

Key features

Hydraulic lifting system

Adjustable height

Detachable sections for easier cleaning and access

Supports lifting, repositioning and transfers

Where it's used

ICUs Hospital wards Rehabilitation centres

Benefits

  • Reduces caregiver strain and risk of back injury
  • Supports safer patient transfers and bedside care
  • Makes hygiene care easier for bedridden patients
