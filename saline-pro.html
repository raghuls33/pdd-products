Saline Pro

Saline Pro is a portable smart IV monitoring system that watches the saline level in an IV bottle and raises an automatic alert when it runs low.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The device is fitted to the IV bottle and switched on.

  2. 02

    It continuously monitors the saline level.

  3. 03

    When the level becomes low, it triggers an alert so staff can replace the bottle in time.

What it is

A compact monitor that attaches to a standard IV setup and tracks the remaining saline continuously. It runs from a 5V power bank, so it keeps working away from a wall socket, including while a patient is being moved.

Key features

Continuous, automatic saline-level monitoring

Low-level alert without manual checking

Powered by a standard 5V power bank

Portable enough to travel with the patient

Where it's used

Hospitals Clinics General wards Patient transportation (ambulances, inter-ward transfers)

Benefits

  • Reduces the need for nurses to check IV bottles by hand
  • Lowers the risk of an IV running dry unnoticed
  • Frees staff time in busy wards and keeps monitoring going during transport
