Camera Slider V2

A compact, motorized camera slider that moves a mobile phone smoothly along horizontal and vertical tracks for steady, cinematic video shots

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The mobile phone is mounted on the holder.

  2. 02

    The user selects the direction of movement (horizontal or vertical) or chooses a preset pattern: triangle, wave, or rectangle.

  3. 03

    The slider moves the phone along the track at the chosen pace while the video is recorded.

  4. 04

    The movement is smooth and repeatable, so the same shot can be captured again with identical motion.

What it is

The slider glides the phone at a controlled pace and can follow preset movement patterns, so creators get professional-looking footage without shaky hands.

Key features

Horizontal and vertical camera movement

Preset movement patterns: triangle, wave, and rectangle

Smooth, steady motion at an adjustable pace

Repeatable camera movement for consistent shots

Where it's used

Content creators Vloggers Product E-commerce videography Short film Photography students Small businesses shooting promotional videos Social media reels Time-lapse Macro shots Educational or classroom video projects

Benefits

  • Produces smooth, professional-looking footage without shaky hands
  • Saves time with automatic, repeatable camera movement
  • Adds creative variety with pattern-based shots
  • Affordable alternative to costly camera motion equipment
  • Easy to set up and use for beginners and professionals alike
