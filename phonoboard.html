Phonoboard

Phonoboard is an interactive learning device designed to make alphabet learning engaging and enjoyable for children

Product prototype

How it works

3 steps · tap a step or press play

  1. 01

    The child presses a letter from A to Z on the device.

  2. 02

    The device plays the corresponding letter sound or phonics audio.

  3. 03

    The selected letter is displayed on the screen for easy recognition.

What it is

The Phonoboard helps young learners recognize letters from A to Z, hear their corresponding sounds, and view them on the display, supporting early language development and foundational literacy skills.

Key features

Interactive alphabet learning from A to Z

Audio playback of letter sounds and phonics

On-screen display of selected letters

Engaging audio-visual learning experience

Where it's used

Preschools Kindergartens Primary schools Learning centers Special learning environments Home-based education to support alphabet recognition Phonics practice Early reading development

Benefits

  • Makes alphabet learning fun and interactive
  • Improves letter recognition and sound association
  • Supports pronunciation and early phonics development
  • Builds foundational reading and language skills
  • Encourages independent learning and repeated practice
  • Helps children develop confidence in early literacy and reading activities
