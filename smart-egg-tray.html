Smart Egg Tray

An intelligent IoT-enabled egg monitoring tray designed to automatically track egg inventory and monitor freshness in real time.

Product prototype

How it works

  1. 01

    The user places eggs in the designated slots of the monitoring tray.

  2. 02

    The device detects the presence of eggs and monitors their quantity using weight measurements.

  3. 03

    It detects gases associated with egg spoilage to help identify potential freshness issues.

What it is

A smart egg tray which gives convenient way to keep track of egg quantities, identify potential spoilage, and maintain better food storage management.

Key features

Automatic egg quantity and inventory monitoring

Real-time freshness and potential spoilage detection

Egg presence verification for accurate inventory tracking

Where it's used

Smart kitchens Household refrigerators Bakeries Poultry farms Supermarkets Food storage facilities Inventory management systems

Benefits

  • Helps monitor egg quantities and freshness conveniently
  • Supports early identification of potential spoilage
  • Reduces food waste by encouraging timely consumption
  • Simplifies grocery planning through automated inventory tracking
  • Improves food safety and storage management
  • Enables remote monitoring through a connected digital dashboard
